ABOUT MECHANICL WATCHES

        A mechanical watch is a timepiece powered by a wound mainspring. Instead of relying on a battery or electronic movement,
        it uses a carefully engineered system of gears, springs, and an escapement to measure time.
        Mechanical watches are admired not only for their ability to tell time but also for the craftsmanship and engineering
        hidden inside their cases.

⚙️ How Does a Mechanical Watch Work?

        A mechanical watch contains hundreds of tiny components that work together.

        1. Mainspring

        The mainspring stores energy when the watch is wound.

        2. Gear Train

        The gear train transfers energy from the mainspring through the movement.

        3. Escapement

        The escapement controls the release of energy and allows the gears to move at a controlled rate.

        4. Balance Wheel

        The balance wheel oscillates back and forth and acts as the timekeeping regulator.

        5. Hands

        The movement eventually transfers controlled energy to the hands, allowing them to display the hours, minutes, and seconds.

🏆 Mechanical Watch Features

When exploring a mechanical watch, you may encounter features such as:

        Power Reserve
         Indicates approximately how long the watch can continue running after being fully wound.

        Skeleton Movement
         Exposes parts of the movement through cutouts in the dial or bridges.

        Exhibition Caseback
         A transparent caseback allows the wearer to view the movement.

        Moonphase
         Displays the current phase of the Moon.

        Chronograph
         Provides stopwatch functionality.

        Tourbillon
         A complex mechanism designed to address certain effects of gravity on the regulating system.
